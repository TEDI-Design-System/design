# Contributing to TEDI design

This repository holds the design work for TEDI: Figma components, their reviews and the Zeroheight documentation. Everyone taking part is expected to follow the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/TEDI-Design-System/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Proposing a new component or improvement

Start a [Discussion](https://github.com/TEDI-Design-System/general/discussions). Describe the need, where it comes up and, if you can, examples. The design team reviews proposals, and once one is approved, opens the design issue here with the "New TEDI-Ready component" or "TEDI-Ready component improvement" template. Those templates walk through design, reviews (design, development, accessibility) and publishing.

## Reporting a problem

- **Design or UX problems in existing components:** open an issue here. Add the component name, a Figma link or screenshot and what goes wrong.
- **Accessibility problems in the design:** use the "Accessibility issue" template.
- **Bugs in the code:** report them in [react](https://github.com/TEDI-Design-System/react/issues) or [angular](https://github.com/TEDI-Design-System/angular/issues).

## Questions

Ask in the [TEDI Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/tedisystem/shared_invite/zt-3hd8tnjf7-Z1smXI~ns0p8E2cxoFUKbQ), on the design channel. See [Getting help](https://github.com/TEDI-Design-System/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md) for other channels.

The design process and Definition of Done are in the [developer wiki](https://github.com/TEDI-Design-System/general/wiki/Definition-of-Done).
